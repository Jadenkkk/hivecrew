HIVE Studio is a Seoul-based creative entertainment collective at the intersection of dance, music, and visual storytelling. What started as a crew became a creative agency with a global lens.

We exist to bridge the gap between Southeast Asian brands seeking cultural authenticity and Korean creators with the talent to deliver it. HIVE is the strategy, the production, and the connection — all in one.
